Support

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Cinemail is made by one person. Write to me and I read it myself - there is no ticket queue and no bot in between. I answer in English or Russian.

Get in touch

The fastest way is Feedback inside the app, at the top of every screen: it attaches the app version and, if you want, a screenshot, so I can see what you saw. Replies come back into the app.

Or by email, any time:

support@dafaspace.com

I aim to reply within two working days. If something is broken and blocking you, say so in the subject line and it goes to the front.

Delete your account

You can do it yourself, immediately, without asking me:

  1. Tap your avatar, top right.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account.
  3. Confirm.

Your films, ratings, notes, lists, friendships and the messages you sent are deleted together with the account, in one step. Nothing is kept behind a "deactivated" flag. If the button ever fails, email me and I will do it by hand.

Take your data with you

Everything you put into Cinemail can come back out at any time, without asking. Tap your avatar and use Download for an Excel file of your films, lists and members, or JSON backup for the complete structured export. Both restore into a new account through Restore.

Coming from somewhere else? Import from Letterboxd and Import from IMDb take the CSV those services export.

Report someone

Cinemail lets people send each other films, notes and messages. If someone sends you something abusive:

  • Remove them. Open Inbox, expand your friends list and remove the person. They can no longer send you anything.
  • Tell me. Email support@dafaspace.com with the person's name and what they sent, or use Feedback in the app with a screenshot. I read reports within one working day and can remove an account outright.

Common questions

A film is missing, or its crew is wrong

Film data, posters and crew come from TMDB, a community-maintained database. If something is wrong there it will be wrong in Cinemail too. TMDB accepts corrections from anyone, and Cinemail picks them up automatically. Tell me as well if a search is failing to find something that clearly exists.

Picks are not personal enough

They are built from what you have rated. Below about fifteen watched films there is not enough to read beyond genre and era, and the app says so on that screen. Rating more, and writing a line about why, sharpens them quickly.

Nothing appears when I search for a friend

Search matches the display name your friend chose, which is not their email. Ask them what name they set under their avatar.

My films did not sync to another device

Sync needs an account. If you started with Start without an account, everything lives on that one device until you join - and joining keeps what you already have. If you are signed in on both and still see a difference, email me.

Does it work offline?

The app opens and your lists are readable without a connection. Searching for a new film, picks and anything involving friends need the network.

Privacy

What is collected, why, and where it is stored is set out in the Privacy Policy. Short version: no ads, no analytics, no trackers, nothing sold.
